# PRODUCT.md — 晨刊 MATINS

Durable product truth. Visual decisions live in `DESIGN.md`; the reasoning behind
both lives in `品牌視覺發想/確定視覺/brand-definition.md`.

## What it is

A personal daily brief that assembles itself at **08:00 UTC+8** and compresses three
subjects onto one page: 財經 podcast (main course) → Product → Design (dessert).
Static HTML, generated by a Python pipeline, served from GitHub Pages. 107 issues
published and archived.

## Who it is for

**One person. n = 1.** The author is the reader.

- **When:** ~08:00, before work. 5–10 minutes, desk or commute, lit room.
- **From → to:** opens X first thing, scrolls a feed with no end, retains nothing,
  leaves carrying "I probably missed something" → 掌握感・不落後: "everything I
  needed to know today, I know."
- **Threshold:** must never exceed ~10 minutes, and must never require the decision
  "should I keep reading?"
- **What kills it:** a morning where the page feels like homework instead of ritual.

Open question, logged in the brand's learning log: whether this ever has a second
reader. Rule: do not sacrifice today's decisions for an imagined audience, but do
not build something so private it cannot be handed to someone.

## The competitor

Not another newsletter — **scrolling X / Threads**. Much of the content is even
harvested from X. The difference is not the material; it is that one of them ends.

## The promise

> 今天該知道的，讀得完。

Completeness and finitude in one line, and falsifiable by the product itself. Two
consequences that look like design choices but are actually product rules:

- **A visible bottom.** No infinite scroll, no "load more", no related content, no
  next-issue autoplay.
- **An empty day is published empty.** Padding a thin day is a breach of the
  promise, not a service to the reader.

Conversion goal: **come back tomorrow morning.** Not signups, not shares, not reach.

## Voice

克制 · 準時 · 有界線 · 誠實 · 不亢奮. Much of it is refusal: it does not cheer, does
not apologise, does not thank, does not nag about a broken streak. The absence of
encouragement is the encouragement — a commitment device that nags becomes something
you avoid.

## Guardrails

Finance is the main course, and that is where a finance product goes wrong:

- Report **what happened**, never **what to do**. No urgency, no implied entry or
  exit.
- Podcast summaries preserve attribution — 「游庭皓認為…」, not 「市場將…」. An
  opinion compressed into an unattributed assertion is this pipeline's most
  dangerous failure mode.
- Never optimise away a hedge to make a summary punchier. The hedge is the content.
- No push notifications. It waits; it does not chase.

## Cost

No transaction exists, but running cost is the honest equivalent: Anthropic + Groq +
6551 API spend, tracked in `usage_log.json`. A monthly ceiling breach is a signal to
prune sources, not to raise the ceiling silently — a ritual that quietly gets
expensive is one that will be cancelled in a hurry.
